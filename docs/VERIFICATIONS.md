# Verifications

Every control's proving injection and every answerable-by-doing check, past and pending. The
acceptance standard is violation injection: a control is proven by deliberately creating the
violation it exists to stop and watching it fire — never by observing that nothing bad happened.

**What this document is, and is not.** It is the test-plan layer: more durable than any one ticket
(a recut ticket inherits its rows; a passed row keeps its evidence pointer), more fluid than the
design (rows are added as controls are added — a new control lands with its injection row). It
records **what must fire and what firing proves**; the how-to detail of running an injection lives
with the implementation. Status values: **proven** (with the date and the record holding the
evidence), **pending** (with the [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) unit that delivers the control, or the
production point at which a drill or manual exercise runs), **parked** (deliberately not run, with
the standing reason). The rows of §§1–4 come in three kinds, and the kind decides whether a proof
stays current or holds only for its date (ADR-0046, via the [decision-record
index](./adr/README.md)). An **automatable** injection becomes a permanent CI test, and its proof
runs continuously from the first proven date. A **drill** on real substrate and a **manual
exercise** are proven as of their date, and re-running either after relevant change is the affected
unit's business, not this table's. Each automatable control's mutation demonstration lives in the
companion ledger, [MUTATIONS.md](./MUTATIONS.md).

## 5. Parked, revisits, and answerable-by-doing

Answerable-by-doing: open empirical questions that need an experiment or accumulated data rather
than a build, each with its trigger point.

| Check | Standing |
| --- | --- |
| **The real Gmail ceiling for this account** — the documented 6,000 units a minute per user per project versus what the account actually tolerates | Answerable only by doing: [production point 1](../ROADMAP.md#production-point-1--the-read-path) is the experiment, watched on the rate gauge; the controller exists because the answer is unknowable in advance |
| **Fastmail's actual throttling behavior** — real limits, `Retry-After` presence | How Fastmail throttles under the system's real workload is answerable only at [production point 3](../ROADMAP.md#production-point-3--a-second-of-everything), and the cost profile's design of starting with a cautious guess and learning the real limits (ADR-0023) assumes they stay unknown until then. Simpler facts, such as whether a `Retry-After` header arrives, can show up when the contract suite runs against Fastmail at [X4](../ROADMAP.md#group-x--expansion) (ADR-0024) |
| **Gate skip rates versus prediction** — whether the composite predicate's skip reasons distribute as designed | Answerable at [production point 1](../ROADMAP.md#production-point-1--the-read-path)'s skip-rate review, from the recorded decisions — the first evidence-based tuning pass |
| **Index drift over days** — whether the index and the provider disagree after days of delta sync running beside backfill | Answerable only by doing: reconcile counts and identities against the provider at [production point 1](../ROADMAP.md#production-point-1--the-read-path). A disagreement is a G4 failure to chase, and the cursor-gap injection in §4 is the control (ADR-0018). It is also the first proof of how the real provider reports a message removed from the mailbox, which the contract suite's run against the real provider cannot show without a delete |
| **The canonical mapping against the real corpus** — whether the canonical model compiles the operator's actual mail without a message, label or thread the mapping cannot represent | Answerable only by doing: the first backfill at [production point 1](../ROADMAP.md#production-point-1--the-read-path) is the experiment, and the fixtures never pretend to carry it (ADR-0044, ADR-0017) |
| **Provider effects of the mutation verbs** — whether what label, unlabel, move, archive, mark read, star, trash and spam do to the real mailbox matches the fake's model of them | Answerable only at [production point 2](../ROADMAP.md#production-point-2--the-agent-acts), the first mutation a deployable makes against the real mailbox. | **The residual's population and skip-reason distribution** — how much traffic the gate skips, and why | Measurable from accumulated gate decisions; revisit once [production point 1](../ROADMAP.md#production-point-1--the-read-path) has run for a while. | **Tier 1–2 recall on real traffic** — the masking miss rate against the operator's actual mail | Accumulates from the masking-events review loop, from [production point 1](../ROADMAP.md#production-point-1--the-read-path) onward. Each migration that grants a privilege is reviewed against the statements it serves |
| **Full compromise drill of a process holding provider credentials** | Parked — not scheduled. The design's answer is hardening plus surviving evidence (ADR-0028), verified piecewise (admission policy at deploy time, the append-only audit grants in §4); whether a full drill is ever worth running is the operator's call |
| **Drive the runaway rule to fire on a deployed system → a person is paged** | Parked. The repository proves the signal and the rule under test, and routing a firing rule to a person is the platform's (ADR-0077, ADR-0051), so the page's proof sits with the deploying side |
| **Push an unsigned deployable image, one that violates the pod-hardening policy, and an unsigned chart artifact → the images are refused before they run, the chart before it installs** | Parked. The refusal is admission machinery, which belongs to the cluster the chart lands on (ADR-0052). Signing the images and the chart is the release workflow's, in [F4](../ROADMAP.md#delivered-mapped-to-outcomes), and whether an unsigned artifact is refused is proven on the deploying side |
| **ADR-0034's recorded-state-only bound** — the status operation reads only recorded tables, never components | Parked as a standalone injection: the bound is the contracts-only pillar's discipline, whose stated limit is that it is truly tested only when an evolution arrives; the operational-state-only bound's injection in §1 covers the observable half.
