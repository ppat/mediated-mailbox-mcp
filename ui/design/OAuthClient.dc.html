This names the app Google shows at each consent and sets who may use it. A new project must do it before it can have a client.
1Check the project picker at the top shows mediated-mailbox-482113. Press Get started.
2Under App Information, enter App name mediated mailbox copy.
3Choose your address as User support email. Press Next.
4Under Audience, choose External. Press Next.
5Under Contact Information, enter your address. Press Next.
6Under Finish, tick the agreement to Google's user data policy. Press Continue, then Create.
Internal is offered only to an organization. With a personal Google account, choose External. Internal works only if every mailbox that connects through this client is in this organization. External always works.
