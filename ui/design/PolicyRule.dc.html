Lift the restriction on fmr.com
In personal, 1 sender and 212 stored messages are restricted by this suffix and by no other rule. From each process's next policy reload, their bodies are no longer denied for their sender. A message scanned before the rule existed, or skipped by the scan gate, can then be released to the agent at once. A message held as restricted goes back to pending scan when a scanning workload next compares the index with the policy, and is released once a scan finds no code or link in it. A body already released cannot be recalled.
This is a base rule. These counts are personal's. Each account's policy screen shows its own. Every account loses it: family, personal, work.

friction follows what a change lifts
· adding a rule or a suffix: no confirmation
· lifting on the account's own rule: this dialog, no typing
· lifting on a base rule: this dialog plus the typed identifier
focus starts on Cancel, is trapped in the dialog, and returns to the control that opened it. the lift control is named by its effect and never says delete. Lift restriction on the rule screen sits apart from Edit, outlined in the restricted color.
after a lift the landing screen reads "Lifted fmr.com. Put it back", which adds it back with no confirmation.
